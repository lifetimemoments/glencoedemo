About the farm

A working farm,
a warm welcome

Set in five acres of beautiful countryside, Glencoe Farm sits peacefully within the ancient Cinque Port town of Rye — overlooking fields towards Winchelsea, yet just a five-minute stroll from the citadel and town centre.

As a working farm it offers a genuine countryside atmosphere: open fields, gardens, and farmland all around. Wildlife is often seen about the place — birds, rabbits, and our resident ducks — so it suits anyone who enjoys nature and a slower pace of life.

  • Peaceful countryside location
  • Five-minute walk to Rye town centre
  • Secure on-site parking
  • En-suite bedrooms with free Wi-Fi

— Carol & Bert, your hosts

Beyond the gate

The perfect base
for East Sussex

With Rye town centre a five-minute walk away and direct trains to Hastings, Ashford, Canterbury and Brighton, there is plenty to keep you busy — if you can bring yourself to leave the farm.

15-min drive

Camber Sands

East Sussex's famous sandy beach — kite surfing, windsurfing, golf and a cycle path running the length of Camber. Dymchurch, Hythe, New Romney and Lydd are just down the road.

On foot

1066 Country Walks

The 1066 trail runs straight through Rye — pass Camber Castle on the way to Winchelsea, or keep walking all the way to Hastings if you're feeling ambitious.

20 min by car or train

Hastings

Explore the quirky Old Town shops and pubs, the renovated pier, Smugglers' caves and the Blue Reef Aquarium — or just wander the beach with fish and chips.

30 min by car or train

Ashford & beyond

A day of shopping at the McArthur Glen Designer Outlet, or connect onwards to Canterbury and Maidstone — the Eurostar can even take you to Paris for the day.
